# Database Design Specification

**Project:** Smart School Management and Academic Information System (SSMS)  
**Target Environment:** Majang Zone Secondary School, Gambella Region, Ethiopia (Grades 9–12)  
**Database Engine:** PostgreSQL (Relational, ACID Compliant)  
**Object-Relational Mapping:** Django ORM  

---

## 1. Relational Database Architecture Overview

The SSMS database is designed according to **Third Normal Form (3NF)** standards to ensure data integrity, eliminate redundancy, and support between 1,000 and 2,000 students across 20+ offline laboratory computers.

### Core Architecture Pillars:
1. **Academic Scaffolding:** Separates physical grade levels and sections from specific academic calendar years using a centralized `ClassOffering` junction.
2. **Three-Part Naming:** Supports native Ethiopian naming conventions (`first_name`, `father_name`, `grandfather_name`) without artificial Western surnames.
3. **Historical Preservation:** Uses `StudentEnrollment` and `PromotionRecord` tables so that a student's past grades, sections, and conduct are preserved forever even after they advance to subsequent grades.
4. **Referential Integrity & Constraints:** Every relationship uses foreign keys with strict deletion behaviors (`PROTECT` or `CASCADE` where appropriate) and compound unique constraints to prevent duplicate enrollments, duplicate attendance entries, or duplicate examination scores.

---

## 2. Explanation of Database Normalization (3NF)

* **First Normal Form (1NF):**
  * Every table has a primary key (`id` generated by PostgreSQL via `BigAutoField`).
  * All attributes are atomic (e.g., student names are split into `first_name`, `father_name`, and `grandfather_name`; addresses and contacts have dedicated atomic columns).
  * No repeating groups (e.g., attendance records and marks are normalized rows in separate tables, not comma-separated lists or hard-coded columns).

* **Second Normal Form (2NF):**
  * All non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key.
  * In composite associations like `StudentEnrollment` and `TeacherAssignment`, attributes such as enrollment status or primary teacher flags describe the relationship itself, while student bio data remains in `Student` and teacher bio remains in `Teacher`.

* **Third Normal Form (3NF):**
  * Non-key columns do not depend transitively on the primary key through another non-key column.
  * For example, a student mark depends on the `ExamSubject` and `StudentEnrollment`. The subject title, credit hours, and maximum possible marks reside in `Subject` and `ExamSubject`, avoiding redundant duplicate storage in each individual student mark record.

## 3. Detailed Entity & Table Catalog (20 Core Models)

### Group A: Identity & Access Control (`accounts`)
#### 1. `Role` (`accounts_role`)
Stores available system roles for role-based access control.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `code` (VARCHAR(50), Unique) - e.g., `PRINCIPAL`, `REGISTRAR`, `TEACHER`, `ADMIN`
* `name` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "School Principal", "Registrar"
* `description` (TEXT, Nullable)
* `is_active` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)

#### 2. `User` (`accounts_user`)
Custom user model extending `AbstractUser`.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `username` (VARCHAR(150), Unique)
* `email` (VARCHAR(254), Blank)
* `password` (VARCHAR(128), PBKDF2 Hashed)
* `role_id` (FK -> `accounts_role.id`, Nullable, on_delete=SET_NULL)
* `phone_number` (VARCHAR(20), Blank)
* `is_active` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)
* `is_staff` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)
* `is_superuser` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)
* `date_joined` (TIMESTAMPTZ)

---

### Group B: Academic Structure (`academics`)
#### 3. `AcademicYear` (`academics_academicyear`)
Represents an academic school year (e.g., 2026/2027 or 2018/2019 E.C.).
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `name` (VARCHAR(100), Unique) - e.g., "2026/2027 (2018/2019 E.C.)"
* `code` (VARCHAR(20), Unique) - e.g., "AY-2026"
* `start_date` (DATE)
* `end_date` (DATE)
* `is_current` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)
* `is_closed` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)

#### 4. `Grade` (`academics_grade`)
Physical secondary grade level.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `name` (VARCHAR(50), Unique) - e.g., "Grade 9", "Grade 10", "Grade 11", "Grade 12"
* `level` (SMALLINT, Unique) - Numerical order (9, 10, 11, 12)
* `description` (TEXT, Blank)

#### 5. `Section` (`academics_section`)
Classroom section division.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `name` (VARCHAR(20), Unique) - e.g., "A", "B", "C", "D"
* `description` (VARCHAR(100), Blank)

#### 6. `Stream` (`academics_stream`)
Curriculum stream specialization.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `code` (VARCHAR(20), Unique) - e.g., `GENERAL`, `NATURAL`, `SOCIAL`
* `name` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "General Secondary", "Natural Science", "Social Science"
* `description` (TEXT, Blank)

#### 7. `ClassOffering` (`academics_classoffering`)
Active class instance in a given academic year.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `academic_year_id` (FK -> `academics_academicyear.id`, CASCADE)
* `grade_id` (FK -> `academics_grade.id`, CASCADE)
* `section_id` (FK -> `academics_section.id`, CASCADE)
* `stream_id` (FK -> `academics_stream.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `max_capacity` (SMALLINT, Default: 50)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(academic_year, grade, section, stream)`

#### 8. `Subject` (`academics_subject`)
Master catalog of subjects taught in the school.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `code` (VARCHAR(20), Unique) - e.g., `MATH-09`, `PHYS-11`, `HIST-12`
* `name` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "Mathematics", "Physics", "Chemistry", "History"
* `description` (TEXT, Blank)
* `is_active` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)

#### 9. `CurriculumSubject` (`academics_curriculumsubject`)
Maps subjects to grade levels and streams with credit weights.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `grade_id` (FK -> `academics_grade.id`, CASCADE)
* `stream_id` (FK -> `academics_stream.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `subject_id` (FK -> `academics_subject.id`, CASCADE)
* `credit_hours` (SMALLINT, Default: 3)
* `is_elective` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(grade, stream, subject)`

---

### Group C: Staff & Teaching Allocation (`teachers`)
#### 10. `Teacher` (`teachers_teacher`)
Professional profile for teaching personnel.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `user_id` (OneToOne -> `accounts_user.id`, CASCADE)
* `employee_id` (VARCHAR(50), Unique)
* `first_name` (VARCHAR(50))
* `father_name` (VARCHAR(50))
* `grandfather_name` (VARCHAR(50), Blank)
* `gender` (CHAR(1), Choices: M/F)
* `qualification` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "B.Ed in Mathematics"
* `specialization` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "Pure Mathematics"
* `joining_date` (DATE)
* `is_active` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)

#### 11. `TeacherAssignment` (`teachers_teacherassignment`)
Maps teachers to specific subjects and class offerings.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `teacher_id` (FK -> `teachers_teacher.id`, CASCADE)
* `class_offering_id` (FK -> `academics_classoffering.id`, CASCADE)
* `subject_id` (FK -> `academics_subject.id`, CASCADE)
* `is_primary` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(teacher, class_offering, subject)`

---

### Group D: Student Information & Academic Progression (`students`)
#### 12. `Student` (`students_student`)
Master demographic profile for students with Ethiopian three-part naming.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `admission_number` (VARCHAR(50), Unique) - e.g., "SSMS/2026/0001"
* `first_name` (VARCHAR(50))
* `father_name` (VARCHAR(50))
* `grandfather_name` (VARCHAR(50))
* `gender` (CHAR(1), Choices: M/F)
* `date_of_birth` (DATE)
* `address` (VARCHAR(255), Blank) - Kebele/Village in Majang Zone
* `emergency_contact_name` (VARCHAR(100))
* `emergency_contact_phone` (VARCHAR(30))
* `emergency_contact_relation` (VARCHAR(50))
* `admission_date` (DATE)
* `status` (VARCHAR(20), Choices: ACTIVE, TRANSFERRED, DROPPED, GRADUATED)

#### 13. `StudentEnrollment` (`students_studentenrollment`)
Historical enrollment record linking a student to a class offering in an academic year.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `student_id` (FK -> `students_student.id`, CASCADE)
* `class_offering_id` (FK -> `academics_classoffering.id`, CASCADE)
* `academic_year_id` (FK -> `academics_academicyear.id`, CASCADE)
* `enrollment_date` (DATE)
* `status` (VARCHAR(20), Choices: ACTIVE, PROMOTED, RETAINED, WITHDRAWN)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(student, academic_year)` -> Guarantees a student is enrolled exactly once per academic year.

#### 14. `PromotionRecord` (`students_promotionrecord`)
Permanent longitudinal record tracking promotion, retention, or graduation.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `student_id` (FK -> `students_student.id`, CASCADE)
* `from_academic_year_id` (FK -> `academics_academicyear.id`, PROTECT)
* `to_academic_year_id` (FK -> `academics_academicyear.id`, PROTECT)
* `from_grade_id` (FK -> `academics_grade.id`, PROTECT)
* `to_grade_id` (FK -> `academics_grade.id`, Nullable, PROTECT)
* `decision` (VARCHAR(20), Choices: PROMOTED, RETAINED, GRADUATED, CONDITIONAL)
* `average_mark` (DECIMAL(5, 2), Nullable)
* `rank_in_section` (SMALLINT, Nullable)
* `remarks` (TEXT, Blank)
* `approved_by_id` (FK -> `accounts_user.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `created_at` (TIMESTAMPTZ)

---

### Group E: Attendance Tracking (`attendance`)
#### 15. `AttendanceSession` (`attendance_attendancesession`)
Represents an attendance roll-call session for a class offering.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `class_offering_id` (FK -> `academics_classoffering.id`, CASCADE)
* `date` (DATE)
* `session_type` (VARCHAR(20), Choices: DAILY, MORNING, AFTERNOON)
* `recorded_by_id` (FK -> `teachers_teacher.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `remarks` (VARCHAR(255), Blank)
* `created_at` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(class_offering, date, session_type)`

#### 16. `AttendanceRecord` (`attendance_attendancerecord`)
Individual student attendance status in an attendance session.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `attendance_session_id` (FK -> `attendance_attendancesession.id`, CASCADE)
* `student_enrollment_id` (FK -> `students_studentenrollment.id`, CASCADE)
* `status` (VARCHAR(20), Choices: PRESENT, ABSENT, LATE, EXCUSED)
* `reason` (VARCHAR(255), Blank)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(attendance_session, student_enrollment)` -> Prevents duplicate roll calls.

---

### Group F: Examination & Results (`examinations` & `results`)
#### 17. `Examination` (`examinations_examination`)
Official exam cycle or continuous assessment period.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `academic_year_id` (FK -> `academics_academicyear.id`, CASCADE)
* `semester` (VARCHAR(10), Choices: SEM1, SEM2)
* `name` (VARCHAR(100)) - e.g., "Semester 1 Midterm Exam", "Continuous Assessment"
* `start_date` (DATE)
* `end_date` (DATE)
* `is_active` (BOOLEAN, Default: True)
* `is_locked` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)

#### 18. `ExamSubject` (`examinations_examsubject`)
Subject configuration within a specific examination.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `examination_id` (FK -> `examinations_examination.id`, CASCADE)
* `grade_id` (FK -> `academics_grade.id`, CASCADE)
* `stream_id` (FK -> `academics_stream.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `subject_id` (FK -> `academics_subject.id`, CASCADE)
* `max_marks` (DECIMAL(5, 2), Default: 100.00)
* `pass_marks` (DECIMAL(5, 2), Default: 50.00)
* `weight_percentage` (DECIMAL(5, 2), Default: 100.00)
* **Constraints:** `UniqueConstraint(examination, grade, stream, subject)`

#### 19. `Mark` (`results_mark`)
Student score obtained on an examination subject.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `student_enrollment_id` (FK -> `students_studentenrollment.id`, CASCADE)
* `exam_subject_id` (FK -> `examinations_examsubject.id`, CASCADE)
* `marks_obtained` (DECIMAL(5, 2), Default: 0.00)
* `is_absent` (BOOLEAN, Default: False)
* `remarks` (VARCHAR(255), Blank)
* `entered_by_id` (FK -> `accounts_user.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `entered_at` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
* **Constraints:**
  * `UniqueConstraint(student_enrollment, exam_subject)`
  * `CheckConstraint(marks_obtained >= 0)`
  * Clean validation: `marks_obtained <= exam_subject.max_marks`

---

### Group G: Security & Accountability (`core`)
#### 20. `AuditLog` (`core_auditlog`)
System-wide audit trail for sensitive administrative operations.
* `id` (PK, BigAutoField)
* `actor_id` (FK -> `accounts_user.id`, Nullable, SET_NULL)
* `action` (VARCHAR(50), Choices: CREATE, UPDATE, DELETE, LOGIN, OVERRIDE)
* `entity_name` (VARCHAR(100))
* `record_id` (VARCHAR(50), Blank)
* `ip_address` (GenericIPAddressField, Nullable)
* `changes_json` (JSONField, Default: dict)
* `timestamp` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
